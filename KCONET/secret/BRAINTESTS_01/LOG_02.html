LOG 02 - NOV 12th 1986 11:24PM

We tried to play the betacarts to get more intel on how the sysytem works

The game was normal at first, as coded. But as we played, around the 10m mark,

things started to glich. Messages were sprawled across the bottom of the screen

in morse code like "LETMEOUT". Eventually, around the hour mark, things started

appearing in the game that were never coded. Weird faceless characters and new

areas like research facilities with medical equipment and saws covered in blood.

It looks like the situation was worse that we thaught. I dont think i will sleep

this week- even though i need all the brain power i can get to cover this up...

Leo Kylilis, CEO OF KCO, NOV 12 1986
